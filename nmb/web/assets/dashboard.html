                3️⃣ Bypass CGNAT / Carrier-Grade NAT Using Direct IPv6 (No Port Forwarding Needed!)
              

              

                What is CGNAT? Many ISPs (especially on LTE/5G mobile, or optical providers) do not provide a unique public IPv4 address to your router, but instead place you behind shared NAT (addresses in the 100.64.0.0/10 range). On CGNAT, traditional IPv4 port forwarding cannot work from the outside.
              

              

                The Solution: NetworkMeasureBox is fully Dual-Stack! Your device has a globally routable IPv6 address: --.
              

              

                In IPv6 there is NO NAT. You simply open your router's IPv6 Firewall (Pinholing) and allow incoming UDP traffic on port 51820 to the box's IPv6 address. Phones on modern 4G/5G mobile networks have native IPv6 and will connect directly to your VPN, bypassing CGNAT completely!
              

            


            
            

              

                4️⃣ Dynamic Public IPv4: Free Automatic Sync via DuckDNS
              

              

                If your ISP periodically changes your public IP address, switch to the DuckDNS tab. Enter your free token and subdomain from duckdns.org. NetworkMeasureBox will automatically keep your hostname updated every 10 minutes, and all your WireGuard/OpenVPN client profiles will automatically connect to your DuckDNS domain!
